2. Information we process

Account and profile

Email address, authentication identifier, display name, age, gender selection, height, weight, preferred units, language, goals, settings, onboarding state, and account status.

Wellness and activity information

Depending on hardware support and your choices, the app may process sleep records and stages, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, blood-oxygen saturation, skin-temperature readings, stress estimates, steps, calories, readiness scores, workouts, routes, distance, pace, and related timestamps. These are general wellness measurements and may be incomplete or inaccurate.

Connected-device information

Ring connection and binding state, Bluetooth-advertised identifiers, battery level, firmware or device information made available by the manufacturer SDK, last connection or synchronization times, and diagnostic information needed to support pairing and synchronization.

Location

If you grant permission and use route-based workout features, the app processes foreground and, where separately authorized, background location coordinates, timestamps, speed, distance, and derived workout routes. Location permission is also required by some operating-system versions for Bluetooth discovery. Denying location may limit relevant features.

Health-platform information

If you enable Apple Health or Android Health Connect, the app may read and/or write the data types shown in the platform’s permission screen. You control these permissions through the operating system and can revoke them there.

Notifications and technical information

Push tokens, app installation/device keys, notification preferences, locale and time-zone information, device/platform details, app version, network request metadata such as IP address, diagnostics, error logs, synchronization markers, and security/rate-limit records.

Local device storage

The app stores preferences, cached wellness data, synchronization queues and markers, ring state, and workout recovery data locally. If you enable biometric sign-in, credentials may be stored using the operating system’s secure storage and protected by the device’s biometric controls.

Website data

This website does not enable analytics, advertising trackers, or non-essential cookies by default. If these are introduced, this policy and any consent mechanism must be updated before deployment.

3. Why we process information

  • Authenticate accounts and provide requested app functions.
  • Connect to supported hardware and synchronize available measurements.
  • Calculate and display wellness summaries, personal baselines, goals, trends, and reports.
  • Record workouts and routes when requested.
  • Synchronize with Apple Health or Health Connect when enabled.
  • Deliver requested reminders, operational notifications, and administrator-supported account operations.
  • Secure, diagnose, maintain, and improve the service.
  • Respond to support, deletion, and rights requests and comply with law.

  • Google Firebase for authentication, user/profile records, configuration, push-token records, and supporting cloud functions.
  • MongoDB Atlas for backend wellness, sleep, readiness, workout, and diagnostic records.
  • Expo and platform push services for push-notification delivery.
  • Apple HealthKit or Android Health Connect only when you enable the relevant integration.
